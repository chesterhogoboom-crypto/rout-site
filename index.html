One email in. Every file where it belongs.

Rout is a file router and job pipeline for small custom shops — cabinetry, millwork, finish carpentry — that already run on Google Drive, Notion and Gmail. A job email comes in; Rout builds the job folder, opens the job record, and routes every file after that into its place.

How it works

You keep Gmail, Drive and Notion. Rout is the routing layer between them.

1

A job email lands

An RFP from a GC, plans from an architect, a homeowner asking "can you build this." You label it, or Rout spots it.

2

The job gets built

A standard numbered folder tree in Drive, a job record in Notion with the key dates, attachments filed and named one consistent way.

3

Every file after that routes itself

Estimates, shop drawings, change orders, POs, invoices and punch lists land in the right folder, and the job record updates. No more "where did that go?"

The job tree, every job, the same

This is the real folder set our own shop runs on. Rout creates it the moment a job starts, and keeps it filled.

  • One name, one place. Job folders are named Client_Type_Date_Project, so Drive search, Notion and your inbox all agree.
  • Nothing leaves your accounts. Files stay in your Drive. Records stay in your Notion. Rout only routes.
  • Humans send, Rout files. It never emails a client or a GC on its own.

Who it's for

Shops that are too big to keep it all in one person's head and too small for enterprise construction software.

A good fit

  • 1 to 15 people. Cabinetry, millwork, finish carpentry, furniture, stairs, countertops.
  • You already use Gmail and Google Drive, and Notion (or want to).
  • Jobs arrive as emails with attachments, and files get lost between estimate and install.
  • You'd rather fix filing once than buy a $400-a-month project platform.

Not a fit (yet)

  • Microsoft 365 or Dropbox-only shops. Google Drive is the file system for now.
  • Shops that want scheduling, payroll or accounting in the same tool. Rout routes files; it doesn't replace your books.
  • General contractors running 50 subs. Rout is built for the trade shop, not the GC.
